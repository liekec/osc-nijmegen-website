BECOME A MEMBER

Join OSCN

Open Science Community Nijmegen is open to everyone who wants to help make research more open, transparent and reproducible — students, PhD candidates, postdocs, and staff alike. As a member you'll get access to our events, newsletter, and a network of like-minded researchers across Radboud University and Radboudumc. You do not need any prior expertise or experience in Open Science to join, and joining does not require you to commit to adopting any particular practices. We’re all just here to learn, share and discuss!

Ready to join?

Fill in the membership form to sign up. We'll ask for a few basic details and a profile photo, so we can add you to our members page. It only takes a couple of minutes.
